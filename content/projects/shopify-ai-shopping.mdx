# Shopify AI Shopping Assistant

A shopping assistant for large Shopify catalogs. Shoppers describe what they want in plain language, and it finds products and fills the cart. It is in progress and has no public demo yet.

## What it does

- Adds a chat widget to the storefront, backed by a Remix API.
- Uses OpenAI tool calling to turn each request into product searches and cart actions.
- Reads products and carts through Shopify's Storefront and Admin GraphQL APIs.
- Still to do: tests, rate limiting and support for more than one store.
