# UHS Mobile Ecosystem

Part of my day job at UHS, where I help run e-commerce for a large hardware catalog. The work spans catalog operations and the company's native shopping apps.

## What it does

- Keeps a large hardware catalog accurate and up to date across the store.
- Supports native iOS and Android shopping apps for customers.
- Sends push notifications that reach shoppers on their phones.
- Tunes on-site search so shoppers can find the right product.
