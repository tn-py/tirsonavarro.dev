# Expo Shopify Storefront

An open-source Expo template that turns a Shopify store into a native iOS and Android app — plug in a `.env` and go.

## Technical Highlights

- **Storefront API**: Product browsing, cart, and checkout driven entirely by Shopify's Storefront API.
- **Native Checkout**: Uses Shopify's native Checkout Sheet Kit for a first-class in-app checkout flow.
- **Customer Accounts & Deep Links**: Supports customer accounts and deep linking out of the box via Expo Router.
