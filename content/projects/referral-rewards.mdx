# Referral & Rewards for Shopify

A Shopify app that turns customers into a referral channel. Shoppers share a link, earn points when friends buy, and redeem points for discount codes. It is still in progress and has no public demo yet.

## What it does

- Tracks referral links, welcome bonuses and points earned on purchases.
- Runs double-points events limited to chosen products, vendors or collections.
- Lets merchants set point expiry, rounding and fraud rules, and adjust balances by hand.
- Shows points and redemption inside customer accounts and on the storefront through widgets.
